Our Story

Crafted with

Otter Kitchens was born from a belief that your home deserves more than a catalogue. Every kitchen, every dressing room, every detail is drawn from the warmth of Mediterranean craft — refined for the contemporary Egyptian home.

Founded by Salma Elsaadany and Marcelle Saweris at Arabella Plaza, New Cairo, we design, produce, and install entirely in-house — so quality is never left to chance.
